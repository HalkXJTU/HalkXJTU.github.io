# Institutional identity assets

- `xjtu.webp`: Xi'an Jiaotong University emblem, downloaded from the university's [official emblem page](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/info/1024/5221.htm). Original: `https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/__local/E/26/06/414ABA4090B26221D9D69928163_9872DAFE_9521.jpg`. Resized to 240 × 239 pixels and encoded as WebP.
- `polimi.svg`: unchanged Politecnico di Milano wordmark from the [official university website](https://www.polimi.it/). Original: `https://www.polimi.it/_assets/4b51f00386267395f41e0940abbcd656/Images/logo.svg`.

Used only to identify the institutions in the XJTU–POLIMI joint education programme. The card tint colours are presentation choices. The two cards do not assert separate admission dates or separate awarded degrees.
